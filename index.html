Fantasy Hockey Analyzer

A companion app for Yahoo Fantasy Hockey that brings over the weekly analysis Yahoo already offers fantasy football managers — and adds a truer way to read the standings.

iOS & Android Built with Flutter Read-only In development

What it does

Three features, built around a single head-to-head category league.

1 True standings

A head-to-head week only tells you how you did against one opponent. This scores every team's week against every other team in the league, producing a full record and rank for the week — so a strong week lost to the league's best team reads differently than a weak week that happened to draw the worst. Records accumulate across the season into standings that reflect performance rather than schedule luck.

2 Weekly recap

A written summary of the league's week: matchup results and the category battles that decided them, standout skaters and goaltenders, the week's luckiest and unluckiest teams measured against the true standings, and any trades or waiver moves. The narrative text is generated by a language model from the week's statistics.

3 Free-agent analysis

Ranks available free agents against your league's own scoring categories rather than generic value — weighting each player by how much they help in the categories where your roster is actually behind your upcoming opponent.

How true standings work

The same week, read two ways.

Data & privacy

What the app reads, and what leaves your device.

Sign-in
You sign in with your own Yahoo account using OAuth 2.0. The app never sees or stores your Yahoo password.
What it reads
Only leagues you already belong to — league settings, teams, weekly matchups and category stats, rosters, standings, and transactions.
What it writes
Nothing. The app is read-only and cannot change your roster, lineup, or league.
Third parties
Weekly recap text is generated by a language model. Team names, matchup results, player stat lines, and transactions are sent to that provider. No Yahoo account identifiers, manager names, or personal information are included.
Storage
Access tokens are stored on your own device. There is no account system and no central server holding your league data.

Status

Where the project is today.

  • In active development, currently used by a single league of fewer than ten managers.
  • Built with Flutter, targeting iOS and Android, with a macOS build used for development.
  • Pending Yahoo Fantasy Sports API access approval.
  • Not yet published to the App Store or Google Play.
